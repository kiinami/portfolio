# Description

I participated with several short stories and opinion articles in Excelencia Literaria, an international literature contest for middle and high school students.

I won the second accessit _ex aequo_ award for one of my opinion articles. Winners are given the opportunity to publish in several established press media, an opportunity I have since taken advantage of on several occasions.
