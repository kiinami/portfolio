# Degree Overview

Erasmus+ stay for my 4th year of my [Bachelor's Degree](timeline/etsii) in Politecnico di Milano, one of the best engineering schools in Europe and the best in Italy.

Although I was taking 4th year courses, the convalidation process and the differences in the education systems between Spain and Italy meant that I had to take courses from the Master's Degree in Computer Science, which in many cases were above my level of expertise. This was a fine opportunity to take advanced courses on my interests and explore specializations that were not available on the University of Seville. All courses were taught in English.

## Key Courses

- **Videogame Design and Programming (Semester 1)**: directed and took part in the development of [Shotō](projects/shoto), a small videogame about sailing, exploring, and talking to rat ladies.
- **Design and Implementation of Mobile Applications (Semester 1)**: designed and implemented [Club App](projects/club), an application for kids and parents of youth organizations.
- **Recommender Systems (Semester 1)**: studied all sorts of recommender systems in depth, and participated in the [course's Kaggle challenge](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/recommender-system-2023-challenge-polimi).
- **Robotics and Design (Semester 2)**: built a robot from scratch, with a special focus on user-centered design. This course was taken along with students from the Design School.

## Extracurricular activities

- Worked remotely for the ES3 research group of the University of Seville (see [here](timeline/es3)) for the whole stay.
- Lent a hand on Fridays at [Geko Club](https://gekoclub.wixsite.com/gekoclub/geko-club), a youth organization in the nearby city of Brescia.
- Resided during the academic year at the MilanoAccademia University Residence and participated in its many cultural and formative experiences, including the [JUMP program](https://jump.rui.it/). The residence held both Italian and international students, so I had the luck of polishing my Italian and English too.
