# Role Overview

I was a research and development part-time fellow in the ES3 research group of the University of Seville, in the RPA (Robotic Process Automation) department. My role was a mix of development, QA, documentation and academic research, depending on the needs of the project.

# Key Achievements

- Collaborated with [*Servinform, S.A.*](https://www.servinform.es/) on 3 projects focused on fintech and generative AI: [CODICE](https://www.servinform.es/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Servinform-CODICE-Publicidad-web-corporativa.pdf), [LINGER](https://www.servinform.es/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Servinform-LINGER.pdf) and [MEGARA](https://www.servinform.es/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Servinform-MEGARA-Publicidad-web-corporativa.pdf).
- Researched extensively in the field of artificial intelligence and generative AI, with a focus on RPA. Collaborated in the paper _["What’s Behind the Screen? Unveiling UI Hierarchies in Process-Related UI Logs"](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-70396-6_15)_.
- Deployed and maintained production applications for theses and research projects with container-based setups in Linux servers.
